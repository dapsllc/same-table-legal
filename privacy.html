Same Table

Privacy Policy

Version 1.0 · 15 September 2026

The short version: Same Table collects nothing at all. There is no account, no sign-in, and no server. Every game runs entirely on your device and works with the device offline. Nothing your family does in this app leaves the phone.

1. Who makes Same Table

Same Table is made by DAPS, LLC ("Dad Apps"). There are no investors and no ad network.

2. What we collect — the full list

Nothing. Specifically:

  • No name, email, age, birthdate, photo, voice, contacts, or location.
  • No account, no password, no profile, no PIN.
  • No analytics, no crash reporting, no advertising identifiers, no cross-app trackers.
  • Game progress (for example, a half-finished board) is saved in your device's own local storage. It never leaves the device and is deleted when the app is deleted.

3. Network

The app makes no network requests of its own. The only connection it can cause is the one Apple or Google makes when you buy or restore a game.

4. Purchases

Paid games are one-time in-app purchases handled entirely by Apple (App Store) or Google (Google Play). They take the payment and they hold the record of it. We never see your name, your email, or your payment details — the app only asks the platform "does this account own this game?" and gets back yes or no. Apple's and Google's own privacy policies govern that transaction.

5. Children

Same Table is for families to play together. Because nothing is collected, there is nothing collected about a child either. A short arithmetic question stands between any child and a purchase.

6. What we never do

We don't sell data (we have none). We don't use ad networks. We don't send notifications to pull you back. We don't market to children.

7. Changes

If this ever changes, we'll update this page and the version note above so you can see what changed.
